# GDG WOW Summit Pune 2025

## Event Overview
The GDG Women of Wonders (WOW) Summit Pune 2025 was a spectacular 2-day event held on March 15-16, 2025 at the Hyatt Regency, Pune. The summit brought together over 500 women technologists, industry leaders, and enthusiasts from across India to celebrate women in technology, share knowledge, and build a stronger community.

## Theme
**"Breaking Barriers, Building Futures: Women Leading Tech Innovation"**

## Day 1 Highlights (March 15, 2025)

### Opening Keynote
The summit kicked off with an inspiring keynote by **Dr. Fei-Fei Li**, Co-Director of Stanford's Human-Centered AI Institute, who spoke about "AI Ethics and Inclusive Innovation." Her talk highlighted the importance of diverse perspectives in building ethical AI systems and set the tone for the entire event.

### Technical Workshops
- **"Advanced Flutter Development"** by **Shams Tabrez**, Google Developer Expert, Flutter
- **"Cloud Native Architecture with Google Cloud"** by **Neha Sharma**, Senior Cloud Architect at Google
- **"Machine Learning for Beginners"** by **Priya Desai**, ML Engineer at DeepMind

### Panel Discussion
"Breaking the Glass Ceiling in Tech" featuring:
- **Anjali Iyer**, VP Engineering at Microsoft India
- **Tanuja Randery**, Managing Director at Google Cloud India
- **Debjani Ghosh**, President at NASSCOM
- **Lakshmi Subramanian**, CTO at Tata Digital

### Networking Lunch
A structured networking session allowing attendees to connect with speakers and other participants based on their areas of interest.

## Day 2 Highlights (March 16, 2025)

### Keynote Address
**Melonie Parker**, Chief Diversity Officer at Google, delivered a powerful talk on "Building Inclusive Tech Communities" that received a standing ovation.

### Technical Sessions
- **"Web3 Development and Blockchain Fundamentals"** by **Richa Joshi**, Blockchain Architect at Ethereum Foundation
- **"Android Development with Jetpack Compose"** by **Vaibhavi Naik**, Android GDE
- **"Building with TensorFlow"** by **Dr. Ruchi Saxena**, Machine Learning Engineer at DeepMind

### Hands-on Hackathon
A 4-hour mini-hackathon where participants worked in teams to solve real-world problems using Google technologies. The winning team built an accessible mobile application for women's safety using Flutter and Firebase.

### Closing Ceremony
- Awards presentation for hackathon winners
- Recognition of community contributors
- Closing remarks by **Mona Gandhi**, Founder of Upraised and former member of Airbnb's early team

## Impact and Feedback

### Attendee Feedback
- 97% of attendees rated the event as "Excellent" or "Very Good"
- 85% reported learning new technical skills they can apply immediately
- 92% made meaningful professional connections during the event

### Community Impact
- 25 new women joined the GDG WOW Pune community as active members
- 15 attendees signed up to speak at future GDG events
- 3 new community projects were initiated during the event
